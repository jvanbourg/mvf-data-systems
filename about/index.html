About Marc Fertout

Turning complex IT and data situations into clear, actionable projects.

Senior expertise, applied directly

Marc Fertout is an engineer and IT and data consultant whose career spans enterprise systems, information architecture, business intelligence, project management and healthcare informatics.

His early career was in software engineering, project direction and IT audit, including real-time systems for defense and aerospace programs. In 2007 he founded and led the healthcare IT consulting business unit of Klee Group in Paris, growing it to more than ten consultants and experts. Since 2016 he has worked as an independent consultant in health informatics and data management for ministries, national and regional health agencies, hospitals, research institutions and health-tech companies across Europe.

Now based in Los Angeles, Marc founded MVF Data Systems to bring that experience to businesses and healthcare organizations that need senior expertise without building a large internal team. Clients work with him directly, from first conversation to delivery.
