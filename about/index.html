Marc Fertout

Founder & Principal Consultant

I’m an engineer and IT and data consultant. My work spans enterprise systems, data architecture, business intelligence, project management and healthcare informatics.

From 2007 to 2016 I founded and led the healthcare IT consulting unit of Klee Group in Paris. Since 2016 I’ve worked independently on health-data programs for ministries, health agencies, hospitals and health-tech companies across Europe.

Now in Los Angeles, I bring that experience directly to businesses and healthcare organizations. You work with me, start to finish, in English or French. I’m fluent in both.
