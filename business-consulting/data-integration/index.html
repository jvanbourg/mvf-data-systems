Data Reconciliation & Systems Integration

Make your ERP, inventory, sales and accounting data agree, and keep it that way.

When the same product, customer or transaction looks different in every system, people stop trusting the numbers and start keeping their own spreadsheets. Fixing it means finding exactly where the data diverges, correcting the cause, and connecting systems so the problem doesn’t come back.

Discuss a Project All business services

What this work covers

Data reconciliation

Trace differences between inventory and accounting, sales and ERP, or locations and head office to their source: master data, units of measure, timing, mappings, or manual adjustments.

Master data & data quality

One reliable version of products, customers, suppliers and locations, with ownership, validation rules and checks that catch problems before they reach the reports.

Systems integration

Connect systems through scheduled imports, APIs or middleware, replacing re-keying and copy-paste between applications with automated, monitored data flows.

How the work is done

  1. Map the systems and flows

    Which system is the source of truth for what, and how data moves (or fails to move) between them.

  2. Find the breaks

    Reconcile samples end to end to locate where and why figures diverge.

  3. Fix the cause

    Correct master data, mappings, integration logic and the process steps that create errors.

  4. Automate and monitor

    Put recurring reconciliation checks and integrations in place, with clear ownership.

Relevant experience

Data normalization, master data management and data quality are at the center of Marc’s project history:

  • Source analysis and data normalization linking the systems of five hospitals to a single national monitoring platform.
  • Assessment of a large database (modeling, quality, master data, interoperability) and normalization of data ahead of migration to a new regional system.
  • MDM and data models for multi-site data warehouses.
  • Data engineering with SQL, Python, REST APIs and workflow tools such as Airflow.
