Frequently asked questions

What types of companies do you work with?

Established, privately held small and midsize companies, typically around $5M to $100M in annual revenue: distributors and wholesalers, manufacturers, multi-location service businesses and family-owned companies whose operations have outgrown their systems. Owners, CEOs, CFOs, COOs and operations or finance directors are usually the people we speak with.

Can you help if our ERP and accounting software don’t agree?

Yes. Numbers that don’t match between systems are usually a systems and data problem: inconsistent item or customer master data, integration mappings, timing differences, or manual adjustments that never flow back. Marc traces where the figures diverge, identifies the root causes, and helps fix the underlying process or integration. Accounting judgments and financial statements remain with your accountant or CPA.

Do you replace our accountant or IT team?

No. MVF Data Systems works alongside your CFO, controller, accountant, IT staff or managed IT provider, and fills the gap between them: the systems and information problems nobody owns. It does not provide accounting, bookkeeping, audit or tax services.

Can you work with our existing software vendors?

Yes, and that is often the most cost-effective route. Marc can work directly with your ERP, accounting, inventory or reporting vendors: define requirements, review proposals, and hold deliverables to agreed acceptance criteria.

Do you implement the improvements or only provide recommendations?

Both. A diagnostic produces written recommendations and a roadmap. Marc can then implement improvements directly, such as reporting, dashboards, data reconciliation and integrations, or oversee the vendors doing the work. There is no obligation to continue after a diagnostic.

Can you work on-site in Los Angeles?

Yes. Marc is based in the Los Angeles area and can work on-site across Los Angeles and Orange counties, from the San Fernando Valley and the Westside to Long Beach and Irvine. Much of the analysis can also be done remotely, and most engagements combine both.
