Questions

What types of companies do you work with?

Privately held and family-owned companies of roughly $5M–$100M in revenue: distributors, wholesalers, manufacturers and multi-location service businesses.

Can you help if our ERP and accounting software don’t agree?

Yes. I trace where the figures diverge (master data, mappings, timing, manual adjustments) and fix the cause. Accounting judgments stay with your accountant or CPA.

Do you replace our accountant or IT team?

No. I work alongside them. I don’t provide accounting, bookkeeping, audit or tax services.

Can you work with our existing software vendors?

Yes. I define requirements, review proposals and hold vendors to agreed acceptance criteria.

Do you implement the improvements or only recommend them?

Both. I can implement the fixes myself or oversee your vendors. There’s no obligation after a diagnostic.

Is the diagnostic free?

The first consultation is free. The diagnostic is a paid engagement, quoted once the scope is clear.

Can you work on-site in Los Angeles?

Yes, across Los Angeles and Orange counties. Much of the work can also be done remotely.

Tell me where your systems let you down.

A few sentences is enough. The first consultation is free.
