Business Systems Diagnostic

Find out what’s broken and how to fix it.

When reports conflict, month-end drags on and every fix seems to create a new workaround, the problem is rarely one piece of software. It is how systems, data and processes fit together. The diagnostic looks at all three and tells you, in writing, what is causing the trouble and what to do about it first.

Request a Diagnostic All business services

When a diagnostic makes sense

  • Inventory, sales and accounting figures don’t agree, and the team spends days reconciling them.
  • Management reporting is assembled by hand and no one fully trusts it.
  • You suspect the company has outgrown its ERP or accounting system but don’t want to replace it on a hunch.
  • A vendor project is late or over budget and you need an independent read.
  • A new CFO, COO or owner wants a clear baseline of how systems and data actually work.

What it is not

The diagnostic examines systems, data flows, reporting and operational processes. It is not a financial audit, an accounting review, or a bookkeeping clean-up, and it doesn’t replace your accountant or CPA. Where findings touch accounting practice, they are written so your finance team and advisers can act on them.

It is also not a software sales exercise. Marc works as an independent consultant, and recommendations favor getting more from the systems you already own whenever that is the better answer.

How it works

The exact scope is agreed in advance and depends on the size of the business and the number of systems involved.

  1. Scope & interviews

    Agree the questions to answer, then speak with the people who run finance, operations, purchasing and IT.

  2. Systems & data review

    Walk through each system, how data enters it, how it moves between systems, and which reports depend on it.

  3. Analysis

    Trace sample discrepancies to their source, map manual steps and workarounds, and assess key processes.

  4. Findings & roadmap

    A written report and a working session to walk leadership through the findings and priorities.

What you receive

  • Written assessment of your systems, data flows, reporting and key processes
  • Key findings and what they mean for the business
  • Root causes behind the most costly data and reporting problems
  • Prioritized recommendations, separating quick wins from larger changes
  • Practical implementation roadmap you can execute with us, your team, or your vendors

What we need from you

Access to the people who use the systems day to day, read access (or a guided walkthrough) of the main systems and reports, and examples of the numbers or processes causing the most frustration.

Nothing has to be cleaned up or prepared in advance. Seeing things as they really are is the point.

Questions about the diagnostic

Is the diagnostic free?

The first consultation is free. The diagnostic itself is a paid, fixed-scope engagement, quoted after that conversation once the scope is clear.

How long does it take?

It depends on the number of systems, sites and processes in scope. The timeline is set out in the proposal before any work begins, and the diagnostic is deliberately focused rather than open-ended.

What happens afterwards?

You own the report and roadmap and can act on them however you choose. If you want help implementing, MVF Data Systems offers systems and reporting improvement projects and ongoing part-time leadership. There is no obligation.
