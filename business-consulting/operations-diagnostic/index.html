Find out what’s broken. Get a clear plan to fix it.

  • 01Written assessmentYour systems, data and processes, as they really are.

  • 04Implementation roadmapExecute it with me, your team, or your vendors.

A diagnostic examines systems, data and processes. It is not a financial audit or accounting review.

Questions

Is the diagnostic free?

The first consultation is free. The diagnostic is a paid engagement, quoted after that conversation.

How long does it take?

It depends on the systems and sites in scope. The timeline is agreed in the proposal before work starts.

What do you need from us?

Time with the people who use the systems, a walkthrough of the main systems and reports, and examples of what’s going wrong. No preparation needed.

What happens afterwards?

You own the roadmap. I can help implement it, or not. No obligation.
