Regional e-health portfolio: data governance

Regional health agency (ARS Bourgogne-Franche-Comté), France · 2020–present
Problem
A regional e-health platform supporting many care pathways needed consistent, governed data for coordination, reporting and analysis.
Marc’s role
Data governance and data architecture consultant.
Work performed
Master data management, data model, data warehouse model, BI and data quality across care pathways including cardiology, obesity, neurology, post-stroke, allergy, geriatrics, oncology and addiction.

MDM and data models, data warehouse model, BI and data-quality framework.

Platform serving 100,000+ patients across 10+ care pathways.
