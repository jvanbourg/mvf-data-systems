A note on experience and context

Marc’s healthcare track record was built mainly with hospitals, health agencies, ministries and medical organizations in France, Luxembourg and elsewhere in Europe. The underlying problems are the same in US healthcare: fragmented clinical data, hard-to-scope integrations, inconsistent reporting and under-managed vendors. The standards he works with (HL7 v2, FHIR, SNOMED CT, LOINC, ICD) are international.

Where a project depends on US-specific regulation, such as HIPAA compliance or CMS program requirements, or on a particular EHR platform’s configuration, Marc works alongside your compliance advisers and vendor specialists rather than in place of them.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help us plan an HL7 or FHIR integration?

Yes. Marc helps define what needs to be exchanged and why: use cases, data flows, the HL7 v2 messages or FHIR resources involved, terminology mapping (for example SNOMED CT, LOINC, ICD-10), data quality rules, vendor capabilities and a phased plan. Interface build is usually done by your EHR vendor or integration team, with Marc defining requirements and overseeing delivery.

Do you work with existing EHR vendors?

Yes, on the client side: requirements, data and workflow analysis, and oversight of vendor deliverables. Marc does not hold vendor-specific certifications for US EHR platforms; his role is independent analysis and oversight rather than platform configuration.

Can you assess clinical workflows before we select new software?

Yes, and it is the right order to do things. Mapping current processes and information flows first produces clearer requirements, better selection criteria and fewer surprises during implementation.

Can you provide independent oversight of an implementation?

Yes. Acting as the client-side owner’s representative is a core part of Marc’s background, including oversight of the prime contractor on a €14M regional digital care program and managing an integration acceptance-testing campaign for a hospital group’s mobile health app.

Do you work with healthcare technology companies?

Yes. Marc has worked with digital health and telemedicine companies on requirements, data models, clinical workflow design and innovation funding applications, and is available as a specialist subcontractor to healthcare consulting firms.

Can engagements be delivered remotely?

Yes. Many healthcare engagements are delivered remotely. Marc is based in the Los Angeles area and can also work on-site across Los Angeles and Orange counties.
