Questions

Can you help us plan an HL7 or FHIR integration?

Yes: use cases, data flows, HL7 v2 messages or FHIR resources, terminology mapping and a phased plan. Your EHR vendor or integration team usually builds the interfaces; I define requirements and oversee delivery.

Do you work with existing EHR vendors?

Yes, on the client side. I don’t hold US EHR vendor certifications; my role is independent analysis and oversight, not platform configuration.

Can you assess clinical workflows before we select new software?

Yes. It’s the right order: clearer requirements, better selection, fewer surprises.

Can you provide independent oversight of an implementation?

Yes. Client-side oversight is core to my background, including a €14M regional digital care program.

Do you work with healthcare technology companies?

Yes: requirements, data models, clinical workflows and funding applications. I also subcontract to healthcare consulting firms.

Is your healthcare experience US-based?

Mostly European (France, Luxembourg and beyond). The standards are international; for US-specific rules such as HIPAA, I work alongside your compliance advisers.

Can engagements be delivered remotely?

Yes. I’m based in Los Angeles and can also work on-site across Los Angeles and Orange counties.
