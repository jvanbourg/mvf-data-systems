Clinical Workflow Optimization

Care pathways, coordination and information flows designed around how care is actually delivered.

New systems rarely fix a workflow that hasn’t been understood. Marc maps how patients, referrals, information and tasks really move between teams and settings, works with clinicians to redesign the process, and turns the result into clear requirements for the technology that supports it.

Typical areas of focus

Care pathways

Multidisciplinary patient pathways across hospital, clinic, home and community settings, including chronic and complex conditions.

Care coordination

Referral handling, care-team communication, back-office and mobile-team planning, and standardized procedures.

Remote monitoring

Telemonitoring protocols combining connected devices with professional supervision, patient engagement and escalation.

Clinic operations

Patient journey, patient information, efficiency, team cooperation and logistics in outpatient and specialty settings.

Clinical indicators

Defining the indicators that show whether a redesigned pathway is working, and the data needed to measure them.

Pre-selection requirements

Workflow-based requirements and selection criteria before choosing or replacing clinical software.

How the work is done

  1. Observe & map

    Interview and observe staff to map the current process, hand-offs and information flows.

  2. Diagnose

    Identify delays, duplicate entry, gaps in information and coordination failures.

  3. Redesign with clinicians

    Build consensus on a better process with the people who deliver care.

  4. Specify & support

    Translate the design into system requirements and support implementation.

Relevant experience

  • Operations management of a care coordination agency: managed information, referral and care-pathway coordination; streamlined internal processes, back-office and mobile-team planning; standardized care procedures.
  • Home management of decompensation crises: co-designed a community care protocol with GPs, nurses, pharmacists and laboratories to manage crises at home without emergency-room visits.
  • Quality of care & clinical performance audit for a private clinic: patient pathway, patient information, efficiency, team cooperation and logistics.
  • IT master plan for a network of health centers: patient pathway continuity and consultation pathway optimization.
  • Home telemonitoring for COPD and sleep apnea: owner’s representative for a program scaled from a 400-patient pilot to 1,000+ patients.
