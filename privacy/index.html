Privacy Notice

Effective November 18, 2026

Information you send us

When you submit a consultation request, we receive the details you enter (name, email address, company or organization, optional phone number and your message) together with the page you submitted it from and, where present, the advertising campaign that brought you to the site (for example UTM parameters or a Google Ads click identifier). We use this information only to respond to your request and to understand which of our marketing is useful.

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service. We do not sell your personal information or share it for others’ marketing.

Health information

Please do not submit patient-identifying information or protected health information (PHI) through this website. The contact form is not designed for it. Describe your organizational or technical needs only; any health data needed for an engagement is handled under a separate written agreement.

Analytics and advertising measurement

We may use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion measurement to understand how visitors use the site and whether our advertising leads to consultation requests. These services use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type and whether a consultation request was completed. They do not receive the contents of your form submission.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we do not load Google Ads measurement. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on and manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings.

Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to maintain business records. You may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have sent us, including under California privacy law, by emailing marc.fertout@mvfdatasystems.com.
